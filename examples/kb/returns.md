# Returns and refunds at Harbor & Pine Supply Co.

Harbor & Pine Supply Co. is a fictional company used only to demonstrate this project.

## What is the return policy?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Clearance items are final sale and cannot be returned.

## How long do refunds take?

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund goes back to the original payment method.

Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

## What if my item arrives damaged or defective?

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 14 days of delivery and include a photo. We will send a replacement or refund the full price.

## Do you adjust prices after a sale?

If an item you bought goes on sale within 7 days of your purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference.

## Do gift cards expire?

Gift cards never expire. Store credit issued for a return is valid for 12 months.
