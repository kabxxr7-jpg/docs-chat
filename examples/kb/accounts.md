# Accounts and support at Harbor & Pine Supply Co.

Harbor & Pine Supply Co. is a fictional company used only to demonstrate this project.

## How do I reset my password?

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email. The reset link we send is valid for 1 hour. If it expires, request a new one.

## How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from Settings. Deleting an account removes your saved addresses and order history, and it cannot be undone.

## When is support open?

Support is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm Eastern. Email replies usually arrive within one business day. We do not offer phone support.

## Can I change or cancel an order?

An order can be changed or cancelled until it leaves the warehouse. After that, you will need to return it.
