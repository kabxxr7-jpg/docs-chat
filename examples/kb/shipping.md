# Shipping at Harbor & Pine Supply Co.

Harbor & Pine Supply Co. is a fictional company used only to demonstrate this project.

## How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the United States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs a flat $14.

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Orders placed after that leave the next business day.

## How much does shipping cost?

Orders over $50 ship free with standard shipping. Smaller orders pay a flat $6 for standard shipping.

## Where do you ship?

We ship only within the United States and Canada. Deliveries to Canada take 7 to 10 business days and may incur customs fees that the buyer pays.

## How do I track my order?

A tracking number is emailed when your order leaves the warehouse. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.
